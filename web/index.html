🧬 Evolvarium — Evolving Artificial Life

An open-ended simulation where you watch synthetic creatures live in an ecosystem and their neural-network brains evolve in real time. Built to observe, tinker, and see evolution happen.

The world

plant (food)   poison   super plant. The world wraps at the edges (leave one side, enter the other).

Species

Herbivores (cyan→blue) eat plants and flee predators. Carnivores (orange→red) hunt other creatures and gain health from kills. Color = diet; a creature's hue shifts as it evolves.

Brain & evolution

Each creature carries a tiny neural network (pure numpy) in its genome. On reproduction the weights mutate; longer-lived creatures reproduce more, so good brains spread by natural selection. The previous action is fed back as a short memory. The starting brains were pre-evolved, so creatures begin already capable.

Training? When does it end?

No separate training step is needed — evolution happens live. And there is no end: the population goes through boom–bust cycles; if everything dies the world reseeds itself. Use ↻ Reset to start over.
